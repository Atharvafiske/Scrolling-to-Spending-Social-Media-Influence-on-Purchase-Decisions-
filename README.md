# Scrolling to Spending: Analyzing Social Media Influence on Purchase Decisions Among Indian College Students Using Regression and Cluster Analysis

## Project Overview

This research project examines how social media influences purchase decisions among Indian youth. With the rapid growth of social media platforms and influencer marketing, understanding consumer behavior has become critical for businesses and marketers.

The study investigates the impact of factors such as Social Media Exposure, Influencer Trust, Social Proof, and Social Media Attitude on Purchase Intention and Impulse Buying Behavior.

## Objectives

- Analyze the relationship between social media engagement and consumer purchase decisions.
- Identify key factors influencing purchase intention among Indian youth.
- Measure the impact of social proof and social media attitudes on impulse buying behavior.
- Segment consumers into distinct groups using Cluster Analysis.
- Provide actionable insights for marketers and businesses.

## Research Methodology

### Data Collection
- Survey-based primary research
- Sample Size: 200 respondents
- Target Audience: Indian Youth
- Structured questionnaire using Likert-scale responses

### Analytical Techniques
- Descriptive Statistics
- Correlation Analysis
- Multiple Regression Analysis
- Cluster Analysis

## Variables Studied

### Independent Variables
- Social Media Exposure
- Influencer Trust
- Social Proof
- Social Media Attitude

### Dependent Variables
- Purchase Intention
- Impulse Buying Behavior

## Key Findings

### Regression Analysis

#### Purchase Intention
- Social Proof showed a significant positive impact on Purchase Intention.
- β = 0.219
- p-value = 0.012

#### Impulse Buying
- Social Media Attitude significantly influenced Impulse Buying Behavior.
- β = 0.250
- p-value = 0.003

### Consumer Segments Identified

Cluster Analysis revealed six distinct consumer groups:

1. Impulse Prone Buyers
2. Moderate Scrollers
3. High Engagers
4. Low Trust Impulsive
5. Passive/Uninfluenced Consumers
6. Intentional Buyers

## Business Implications

- Brands should leverage social proof to improve purchase intention.
- Positive social media experiences can increase customer engagement and impulse purchases.
- Consumer segmentation can help marketers design targeted campaigns.
- Influencer and community-driven marketing strategies can improve conversion rates.
